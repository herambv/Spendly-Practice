Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 27, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we store the expense records you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide. We do not collect payment information or any data beyond what is necessary to operate the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the Spendly service. This includes displaying your expense history, generating summaries, and enabling filtering by date or category. We do not use your data for advertising purposes and we do not sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and is accessible only to you through your account. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information from unauthorized access. You may delete your account at any time, which will permanently remove all associated data from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly uses Google Fonts to load typefaces. This may result in a request to Google's servers when you visit the site, subject to Google's own privacy policy. Beyond this, we do not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us. We are committed to addressing any privacy-related inquiries in a timely manner.
